# 🏎️ The Theme: Life-or-Death Decisions in Fractions of a Second
Whether it is an emergency room physician prioritizing critical care, a mission control specialist navigating deep-space anomalies, or an F1 race engineer making tire strategy calls at 300+ km/h—they all require parsing massive data streams instantly. Your goal is to build Gemma-powered solutions that bring order to this chaos.

## 🏥 Track 1: Clinical Triage (Healthcare under Pressure)
In the emergency room, seconds save lives. Build Gemma-powered systems to help healthcare workers prioritize patients, parse complex medical histories instantly, or automate diagnostic decision-support under extreme clinical constraints.

> Ideal for: AI agents, RAG on medical documentation, and localized clinical assistant tools.

## 🚀 Track 2: Trajectory & Orbit (Deep Space Navigation)
Deep-space exploration offers zero margin for error. Build tools that assist mission control or spacecraft systems in real-time telemetry analysis, orbital anomaly detection, or autonomous navigation when communicating with Earth is delayed.

> Ideal for: Multimodal vision tools, predictive math/science physics assistants, and edge-deployed models.

## 🏁 Track 3: Pit Lane Telemetry (Formula 1 Strategy)
With hundreds of data points streaming from an F1 car every second, race engineers must predict tire degradation, engine thermals, and overtaking windows instantly. Build Gemma applications that turn raw telemetry streams into winning race strategies.

> Ideal for: Function-calling agents, real-time predictive analytics, and conversational crew-chief interfaces.
